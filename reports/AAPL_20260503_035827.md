## 📚 Educational Notes

| Term              | Plain-Language Explanation |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **P/E Ratio**     | Price-to-Earnings ratio. Shows how much investors pay per dollar of earnings. Higher = more expensive. |
| **RSI**           | Relative Strength Index. Momentum oscillator 0–100. >70 = overbought; <30 = oversold. |
| **Moving Average**| Average price over N days. Helps smooth noise and identify trends. |
| **Volatility**    | Annualised standard deviation of returns. Higher = bigger price swings. |
| **Debt-to-Equity**| Ratio of total debt to shareholders' equity. Higher = more leveraged. |
| **Beta**          | Sensitivity to market moves. Beta>1 = amplified swings vs the market. |
| **ROE**           | Return on Equity. How much profit per dollar of shareholders' equity. |
| **MACD**          | Moving Average Convergence Divergence. Trend-following momentum indicator. |
